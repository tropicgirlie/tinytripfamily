# Product

## Register

product

## Users

TinyTripIndex trip planners are family organizers who do the research, compare options, pay deposits, and keep everyone informed. Guests are family members who need a simple shared view with dates, countdown, flights, activities, family details, and the final villa reveal.

## Product Purpose

This product creates branded trip workspaces for group family holidays. The host can search, compare, personalize, and pin a final plan. Guests receive a cleaner shared link with the information they need, without the host's planning controls.

Success means the organizer feels in control and the family does not need long message threads to understand the plan.

## Brand Personality

Warm, organized, playful.

The product should feel like a trusted family planning notebook with enough polish to become a TinyTripIndex sub-product. It can use delight, reveal moments, and social touches, but the core feeling should remain calm and useful.

## Anti-references

- Generic travel affiliate pages that push listings before helping the organizer decide.
- Dense admin dashboards that expose every control to guests.
- Overly beige or generic AI travel pages with soft cards everywhere.
- Full room-booking systems inside the family planner; room preferences are enough for now.

## Design Principles

- Separate host work from guest information.
- Make the pinned decision feel official.
- Personalize by family context, especially ages and child needs.
- Keep reveal moments playful, but never at the cost of clarity.
- Preserve TinyTripIndex attribution while letting each trip feel personally branded.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Text contrast should be comfortable for mixed-age family use. Motion should respect reduced-motion preferences. Guest view should be understandable without needing the host's planning context.
