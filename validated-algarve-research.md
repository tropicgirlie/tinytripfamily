## Confirmed Base: Albufeira

### Albufeira / Olhos de Agua

Best for:

- Bigger holiday atmosphere.
- More restaurants, bars and resort infrastructure in winter.
- New Year's Eve.
- Families who want more going on around the Christmas/New Year period.

Validated notes:

- Algarve winter guides commonly recommend larger resort towns because smaller resorts can be quiet in low season.
